# Workflows

How work moves here, in the smallest number of steps that are actually true.

- **A change.** From an idea to merged.
- **A review.** Who reads it, what they are looking for, what blocks.
- **A release.** What has to be true before it ships.
- **An incident.** Who is told, in what order, and what gets written down.

Describe the workflow that is followed, not the one that ought to be. A
workflow record that disagrees with practice teaches agents the wrong thing.
