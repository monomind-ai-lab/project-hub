# Owner's window

Your room. Future projects, reflections, ideas, drafts, half-formed arguments
with yourself — anything, in any shape, with no schema and no budget.

Three negatives hold it: **never pushed** (push copies `global/` and
`blueprint/` and nothing else), **never linted** (the doctor skips this folder
entirely — a place to think stops being one the moment it reports errors), and
**never pulled into** (`pull` writes only to `projects/<id>/pulled/`).

The way out is promotion: an idea that matures becomes a `blueprint/EPIC.md`, a
record in `global/`, or a `MARK.md` for a repository that does not exist yet.
Freeform in, structured out. Nothing leaves here by itself.
